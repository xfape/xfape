The value to the bank → The de-risked plan

The sixty-second version

If you read nothing else.

The gap
We run on tribes — conventional, digital, finance — each with its own systems. Processes are trapped inside them, and we cannot trace a dollar from disbursement to the income stream it lands in. We are not built for AI agents, and our cost base shows it.
The move
Build one rail our processes run on, where every step is attributed at the source. Attribution gives finance the truth today; the same rail makes us agent-ready — so agents can take routine middle-office work tomorrow.
The prize
A structurally lower cost-to-income ratio, real operating leverage (grow revenue without growing headcount), sharper capital allocation, and a credible "AI-ready" valuation story.
The ask
A 90-day, single-product-line proof with a small budget — and a mandate to lead this transformation across the tribes as CIO.

Why now, and the cost of waiting

The capability arrived. The window is the advantage.

✓ Why this is the moment

Agents can finally take real, multi-step action reliably. The technology that blocked this for years is no longer the constraint — execution and governance are. Moving now means we define our cost structure before competitors define theirs.

✕ The cost of waiting

Every year we don't instrument our processes, the attribution gap widens and the cost base hardens. Competitors who become agent-ready first will out-scale us at lower cost — and the market will price the difference. This is cheaper to start small now than to retrofit later.

The de-risked plan

Small, sequenced, and reversible — each phase earns the next.

No big-bang transformation, no rip-and-replace. We prove it on one product line, then expand only where the data justifies it.

PHASE 1 · 90 DAYS

Attribution proof

One product line. Live disbursement → income-stream attribution for finance. Small budget, measurable result.

PHASE 2

The rail & analytics

Turn the tagged events into a shared rail and analytics. Bring a second tribe's process onto it.

PHASE 3

Generate processes

Use the data to standardise and improve processes, deployed back onto the rail.

PHASE 4

Automate middle office

Hand routine lanes to governed agents — one at a time, attributed and reversible.

🛡️ At every phase: no systems replaced, humans keep the judgment calls, and every action is reversible and audited. We can stop after any phase having already banked the value of the last.
